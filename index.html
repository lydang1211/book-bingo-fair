❓ How to Play
1

📝 Recommend a book

Click "+ Add Your Book" at the top. Pick a bingo category, write a micro-review (1–3 sentences: "This book made me ___"), and submit. You earn 2 points instantly.

2

👍 Like books you love

Browse the Book Fair. If something resonates, hit 👍. Every like earns the recommender 0.5 points. No cap — keep liking!

3

📖 Borrow & Return

Hit "📖 Want to Borrow" to signal interest, then grab the physical copy from the recommder. After reading, come back and post a return review. You and the recommender each earn 2 points.

4

🎴 Complete your Bingo Card

Head to the Bingo Card tab and mark each category as you post for it. Complete any row, column, or diagonal (4 squares) to finish a line!

5

📌 Key rules

Duplicate book posts are welcome. "Want to Borrow" is a social signal only. The returned review is what earns points. No rating system — share freely, judgment-free!
