❓ How to Play
1

📝 Recommend a book

Sign in → click "+ Add Your Book". Pick a bingo category, write a micro-review and optionally a full review. You earn 2 points instantly.

2

🏛️ Browse the Library

Don't have a book? Check the Library tab — books physically available at the office. Hit "I'm Reading This" to borrow, then return and post your recommendation!

3

👍 Like books you love

Browse the Book Fair. Hit 👍 on anything that resonates. Every like earns the recommender 0.5 points.

4

📖 Borrow & Return

Hit "📖 Want to Borrow" on a Fair post, then borrow from your colleague. After reading, post a return review for +2 pts each.

5

🎴 Complete your Bingo Card

Sign in → Bingo Card tab → click a square → link to your recommendation post. Complete any row, column, or diagonal → claim +5 pts. Full card → +20 pts + 🎴 Bingo Master!

6

📌 Key rules

Each recommendation post can only be linked to one bingo square. Duplicate book posts welcome. No rating system — share freely, judgment-free!
