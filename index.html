❓ How to Play
1

📝 Recommend a book

Click "+ Add Your Book". Pick a bingo category, write a micro-review (tagline) and optionally a longer full review. You earn 2 points instantly.

2

👍 Like books you love

Browse the Book Fair. Hit 👍 on anything that resonates. Every like earns the recommender 0.5 points.

3

📖 Borrow & Return

Hit "📖 Want to Borrow" to signal interest, then grab the physical copy from the recommender. After reading, post a return review. You and the recommender each earn 2 points.

4

🎴 Complete your Bingo Card

Mark categories as you post for them. Complete any row, column, or diagonal to finish a line!

5

📌 Key rules

Duplicate book posts are welcome. "Want to Borrow" is a social signal only. No rating system — share freely, judgment-free!
